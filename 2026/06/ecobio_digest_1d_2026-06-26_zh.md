### 鲑鱼养分输入时空差异重塑河岸真菌群落

研究通过DNA宏条形码技术，分析了美国阿拉斯加西南部河岸森林中三种不同鲑鱼源氮输入模式对真菌群落的影响。研究发现，分解中的鲑鱼残体增加了腐生真菌的多样性，而长期的残体迁移处理则降低了特定外生菌根真菌的多样性。沿溪流的自然氮梯度上，外生菌根真菌的反应在功能群和属内差异显著。

- **为什么值得关注**：该研究揭示了海洋源养分输入如何通过时空差异，深刻影响河岸森林生态系统中关键分解者和共生真菌的功能与分类组成。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究基于相关性分析，真菌功能性状与养分获取机制间的直接因果联系尚待验证。

### 大豆根瘤菌趋化性机制解析

研究通过基因敲除实验，明确了在大豆共生固氮菌USDA110中，che2操纵子是驱动其向大豆种子分泌物趋化的主要系统。该菌在软琼脂中的趋化能力丧失，并不影响其在水介质中的游动速度。这为理解该重要农业共生菌如何协调多个化学感应系统以定殖宿主根部提供了新见解。

- **为什么值得关注**：阐明关键固氮共生菌的趋化机制，有助于优化其根际定殖效率，对可持续农业有潜在意义。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究主要在实验室条件下进行，与复杂田间土壤环境的关联尚需验证。

### Dominikia tatrensis Blaszk., Zubek, Niezgoda & B. T. Goto 2026, sp. nov.

Dominikia tatrensis Błaszk., Zubek, Niezgoda & B. T. Goto sp. nov. Fig. 4 A – H; Suppl. materials 1, 3, 5 Typification. Poland • Małopolskie Voivodeship, glomerocarps from a trap pot culture inoculated with rhizosphere soil and root fragments of plants growing in the Tatra 2 / 1 op site located in the lower part of the northwestern part of the Polana Upław meadow, 1298 m asl (49°15'3.6"N, 19°52'46.92"E), 11 May 2023, A. Ronikier and P. Janik (holotype: slide with spores ZT Myc 0067491; isotypes: slides with spores nos. 4012–4017, LPPDSE). GenBank: 45 S: PX 641494 – PX 641499; rpb 1: PX 570049 – PX 570052. Etymology. Latin, tatrensis, referring to the Tatra Mountains, where specimens of this species were originally found. Diagnosis. Differs from (A) D. aurea, the closest phylogenetic relative (Fig. 2; Suppl. materials 1, 3, 5) in (i) the organization of spores in glomerocarps, (ii) the spore wall structure, (iii) the durability of spore wall layer 1, forming the spore surface, (iii) morphometric features of the spore wall, subtending hypha and its pore, (iv) the origination of the septum occluding the pore, and (v) nucleotide composition of sequences of the 45 S nuc rDNA region and the rpb 1 gene, and (B) Glomus fuegianum, the morphologically most similar species, in the formation of glomerocarps lacking peridium and glebal hyphae, as well as in the spore wall structure (see “ General data and phylogeny ” and “ Discussion ” for details). Description. Forming compact hypogeous single and fused glomerocarps without a peridium; each glomerocarp globose to subglobose, 154–280 µm diam, to ovoid, 166–190 × 213–291 µm, with five to twelve radially arranged glomerospores (= spores); fused glomerocarps with two to six single glomerocarps; each glomerocarp probably formed from a swollen cell (characterized below) produced at the tip of a hypha branched from a parent hypha continuous with an extraradical mycorrhizal hypha (Fig. 4 A – C). Spores glomoid, arising blastically at tips of subtending hyphae branched radially from a centrally located swollen cell, 22.3 × 28.0 µm wide with a wall 3.9–5.6 µm thick (Fig. A, C). Spores greyish yellow (4 B 4–4 B 6); globose to subglobose; (48 –) 58 (– 61) µm diam; rarely ovoid; 42–60 × 52–66 µm; with one subtending hypha (Fig. 4 A – H). Spore wall composed of four layers (layers 1–4), including three permanent, smooth layers (layers 2–4; Fig. 4 D – H). Layer 1, forming the spore surface, mucilaginous, flexible to semi-flexible, hyaline, (0.8 –) 1.0 (– 1.6) µm thick when intact, slowly deteriorating with age, usually present as a highly decomposed structure even in older spores (Fig. 4 D – H). Layer 2 uniform (without visible sublayers), semi-flexible, hyaline, (0.8 –) 1.5 (– 2.2) µm thick, tightly adherent to layer 3 (Fig. 4 D – H). Layer 3 laminate, semi-flexible, greyish yellow (4 B 4–4 B 6), (1.8 –) 2.3 (– 3.0) µm thick, consisting of very thin, <0.5 µm, sublayers tightly adherent to and not separating from each other even in vigorously crushed spores (Fig. 4 D, E, G). Layer 4 uniform, semi-flexible, greyish yellow (4 B 4–4 B 6), (0.6 –) 0.8 (– 1.2) µm thick, usually rarely and only slightly separating from the lower surface of layer 3, usually easy to detect (Fig. 4 D, E, G). Only layer 1 stains pale red (7 B 3) to light brown (7 D 6) in Melzer’s reagent (Fig. 4 D, E, H). Subtending hypha greyish yellow (4 B 4–4 B 6); straight or recurved, cylindrical, rarely funnel-shaped, (11.2 –) 14.6 (– 18.8) µm wide at the spore base (Fig. 4 C, E – H). Wall of subtending hypha greyish yellow (4 B 4–4 B 6); (3.4 –) 6.5 (– 8.6) µm thick at the spore base; composed of four layers continuous with spore wall layers 1–4 (Fig. 4 F, G, H). Pore (0.8 –) 1.4 (– 2.0) µm diam, usually occluded by a curved septum continuous with spore wall layer 4; septum 0.8–1.0 µm thick, located at or 3.5–4.0 µm below the spore base (Fig. 4 F – H). Germination unknown. Ecology and distribution. In the field, D. tatrensis probably formed arbuscular mycorrhizal symbiosis with plant (s) of the Tatra 2 / 1 op site (Suppl. material 6). No molecular analysis was performed to confirm the existence of the symbiosis. BLASTn queries sent to GenBank and EUKARYOME showed many environmental sequences with> 96 % identity to sequences of the new species. However, BI and ML analyses of the 45 SD alignment extended by the environmental sequences showed that only five of them clustered with D. tatrensis sequences (data not shown), suggesting their origin from the new species. The sequences were EUK 1633193, EUL 1631679, HE 775327, HE 775330, and JX 096576 with 97.8 %, 98.3 %, 98.39 %, 98.52 %, and 96.55 % identities, respectively. The sequences EUK 1633193 and EUL 1631679 were obtained from Estonian temperate shrubland and temperate broadleaf forest soils, respectively. The HE 775327 and HE 775330 sequences were obtained from roots of Brachypodium pinnatum (L.) P. Beauv. growing in a meadow of North Bohemia, Czech Republic. The JX 096576 sequence was obtained from a soil sample collected from the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, China. These data suggest that D. tatrensis has a widespread global distribution, although it appears to occur rarely.

## 生物炭对土壤氮循环与温室气体排放的影响

**本节要点：**

- 鸡粪生物炭提升土壤肥力与氮素利用效率
- 生物炭显著降低土壤N2O排放
- 高剂量生物炭（7 t ha⁻¹）综合效益最佳

### 鸡粪生物炭对白菜生长、氮素利用及N2O排放的影响

本研究评估了鸡粪生物炭（CMBC）对旱地土壤中泡菜白菜生长、氮素利用效率和氧化亚氮（N2O）排放的影响。实验发现，施用7 t ha⁻¹的CMBC能显著改善土壤理化性质，提高氮素农学效率和表观回收率，并比单施化肥处理减少59.2%至72.1%的N2O排放。结果表明，高剂量CMBC是提升土壤肥力、氮素利用效率并同时减少温室气体排放的有效策略。

- **为什么值得关注**：该研究为集约化蔬菜生产系统中，利用有机废弃物改良剂协同实现作物增产与温室气体减排提供了实验依据。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究为短期盆栽实验，长期田间效应及不同土壤类型下的表现仍需验证。

## 微生物标记物与抗病育种

**本节要点：**

- 鉴定出跨土壤一致的抗根腐病真菌标记物
- 根际真菌群落受基因型和土壤共同影响
- 为微生物组介导的抗病育种提供依据

### 跨土壤鉴定豌豆抗根腐病真菌标记物

研究在四种不同土壤中，分析了八个抗根腐病能力不同的豌豆基因型的根际真菌群落。结果发现，一个与Dactylonectria属100%相似的OTU（OTU3）在抗性基因型中普遍富集，且与种子出苗率正相关。该OTU被确定为跨土壤一致的微生物抗性标记物。

- **为什么值得关注**：为通过微生物组介导的育种策略，培育抗土传病害的豆科作物提供了直接证据和潜在靶点。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究在受控条件下进行，田间验证和功能机制有待深入。
