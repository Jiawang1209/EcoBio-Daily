### 光强对西洋参生长及土壤微生物的影响

在降低光空间异质性的林下环境中，研究光强对西洋参生理、土壤生化性质及微生物群落的影响。结果表明，13.1%的透光率处理下，西洋参净光合速率和叶绿素含量增加，根干重显著提高。同时，土壤脲酶活性、pH和碱解氮含量升高，并与特定pH相关微生物及反硝化细菌的相对丰度变化相关联。偏最小二乘路径模型分析显示，光强通过调节植物生理特性直接促进生长，并通过改善土壤生物与化学性质间接发挥作用。

- **为什么值得关注**：为林下西洋参栽培的光环境管理及农林业系统可持续发展提供了基于土壤微生物视角的实践依据。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究为特定林下生态系统中的田间实验，结果外推需谨慎。

### Mechanisms of soil nitrogen conservation in a degraded karst ecosystem of Southwest China

Distinctive geological and hydrological characteristics are widely believed to promote a leaky nitrogen (N) cycle in karst regions. This paradigm has long shaped our understanding of the low soil N recovery and pronounced N limitation observed in degraded karst ecosystems; Paradoxically, however, previous studies have shown that these systems exhibit a low potential for N loss. Thus, an unknow mechanism may contribute to soil N conservation in degraded karst ecosystems. Here, we quantified hydrologic and gaseous N losses in karst grasslands and grass-shrublands of Southwest China through a comprehensive field monitoring campaign. An in-situ 15 N tracing experiment, combined with measurements of soil gross N transformation rates, was conducted to elucidate the underlying mechanisms of N conservation. Our results confirmed consistently low N losses, with annual fluxes of 0.5 kg N ha −1 y –1 for nitrous oxide, 0.6 kg N ha −1 y –1 for ammonia, and leaching losses of 0.5 and 0.3 kg N ha −1 y –1 for ammonium and nitrate, respectively. Less than 1 % of the experimentally applied 15 N was lost via these pathways. We identified three complementary mechanisms governing N conservation: (i) elevated microbial N assimilation driven by strong microbial N demand; (ii) substantial abiotic N adsorption promoted by high clay and exchangeable calcium content; and (iii) efficient plant N uptake induced by N limitation. Collectively, these processes enhance ammonium retention and utilization while reducing nitrate production and availability, thereby minimizing N loss via nitrification, denitrification, and leaching. This study provides the first systematic, ecosystem-scale evidence that degraded karst systems can possess a high internal N retention capacity. These findings necessitate a rethink of the prevailing leaky N paradigm and provide new insights into how N cycling controls ecological succession in these vulnerable regions.

### Distribution, Characterisation, and Reclamation of Waterlogged Soils: A Review

Waterlogged soils represent one of the most challenging constraints to global food security and sustainable land management, affecting an estimated 640 million hectares worldwide. This review synthesises current knowledge on the global distribution, paedogenic characterisation, and reclamation strategies of waterlogged soils. It examines geomorphological and climatic drivers of waterlogging across major agroecological zones, analyses soil morphological, physical, chemical, and biological characteristics under prolonged saturation, and critically evaluates established and emerging reclamation technologies. Evidence from over 150 peer-reviewed studies published between 1990 and 2024 was integrated. The findings indicate that waterlogging-induced anaerobic conditions trigger cascading redox reactions, including iron and manganese reduction, denitrification, and methanogenesis, which substantially alter nutrient cycling, soil structure, and microbial communities. Reclamation success depends on accurate diagnosis of the cause of waterlogging, integration of drainage engineering with biological and chemical amendments, and long-term monitoring. Future directions include precision drainage design, climate-adaptive management, and the use of remote sensing for large-scale waterlogging mapping. This review provides a reference for soil scientists, agronomists, and land-use planners managing waterlogged landscapes.

### 蛇莓覆盖对梨园土壤微生态的影响

研究评估了果园覆盖蛇莓对土壤微生态的影响。结果表明，覆盖改善了土壤理化性质，且效果随覆盖时间延长而增强。覆盖调控了土壤微生物群落结构，但优势菌门（变形菌门、子囊菌门）未变。代谢组学分析发现，覆盖土壤中“植物次生代谢物生物合成”等通路显著富集，花生四烯酸被鉴定为潜在关键代谢物。

- **为什么值得关注**：为果园“以草治草”的生态管理模式提供了土壤微生物和代谢层面的理论依据。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究在特定果园进行，其结论在不同气候、土壤类型或果树品种下的适用性需进一步验证。

### 新型一氧化氮歧化酶驱动好氧反硝化

研究从农田和稻田土壤等样品中富集好氧反硝化菌，并鉴定出新型一氧化氮歧化酶基因。构建的基因工程菌表现出72.6–85.4%的一氧化氮降解效率。对粗酶液的酶活分析显示其符合米氏动力学特征。该酶能将一氧化氮歧化为氮气和氧气，从而减少氧化亚氮排放。

- **为什么值得关注**：发现并验证了新型一氧化氮歧化酶的功能，为利用好氧反硝化技术减少废水处理过程中的温室气体排放提供了关键酶学基础。
- **证据类型**：实验
- **限制**：研究主要在实验室条件下进行，该酶在实际复杂废水环境中的稳定性和处理效能有待工程化验证。
